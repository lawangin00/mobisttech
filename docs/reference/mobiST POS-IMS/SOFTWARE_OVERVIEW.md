# mobiST POS - Software Overview

**Retail POS & Inventory Management System**
Version 1.0.0
Developed by mobiST Technologies
Effective: 6 September 2026

## What mobiST POS is

mobiST POS is a Windows desktop Point of Sale and Inventory Management System designed for mobile-phone, tablet, accessory and similar retail businesses.

The product is offline/local-first. Each shop operates its own installation and local database. Normal retail work does not depend on a continuous internet connection.

## Core capabilities

mobiST POS supports:

- Product catalog and inventory management.
- Serialized devices such as phones/tablets with IMEI or serial tracking.
- Quantity-based stock such as accessories.
- Purchases, suppliers and buying sources.
- Fast POS sales with split-payment allocation.
- Customer records and transaction history.
- Invoices, print/PDF output and optional WhatsApp/email sharing.
- Returns and exchanges.
- Expenses, sales reporting, profit reporting and inventory reporting.
- Verified local backup and guarded restore.
- Optional shop-owned Google Drive backup.

## Roles and access

V1 uses three roles: Owner, Manager and Salesperson. Privileged settings, user administration and Google Drive connection are restricted to the Owner where applicable.

## Data and privacy model

Business data is stored on the shop's local Windows PC. mobiST Technologies does not operate a central operational database for normal POS data.

Online features are optional. When enabled by the shop, selected information may leave the local PC only for the requested function, such as invoice sharing or Google Drive backup.

Google Drive backup connects the shop's own Google account through browser-based OAuth authorization. Backups are stored in that shop's Google Drive, not in a mobiST Technologies company Drive account.

## Licensing

mobiST POS supports a built-in 7-Day Trial and signed paid licenses for 1 Year, 5 Years, 10 Years and Lifetime terms. Paid licenses are device-bound and are validated offline after activation.

## Platform

Windows 11 x64 is the primary supported platform. Windows 10 x64 and Windows 10 x86/32-bit are compatibility-tested targets subject to the documented support limitations of those operating systems.
